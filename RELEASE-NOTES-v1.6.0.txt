Tech Kadak v1.6.0 - AI Helper update

What's new:
- AI Helper: chat (or speak) with Gemini and it finds apps for you from the Tech Kadak store. Uses your own free Gemini key, kept only on your phone.
- Voice: tap the mic to ask by voice, and turn on the speaker switch to hear the answers.
- New apps alert: you now get a notification whenever new apps are added (one summary if several arrive together).
- Device sharing (optional, off unless you allow it): lets the app owner see your phone name and the names of your installed apps to improve suggestions. You can change this any time in Settings.
- Admin panel: the owner can now see opted-in devices and their installed apps.

Everything else works as before. Your data stays safe.
