# Privacy

What Tech Kadak stores, in plain words.

- **Anonymous usage stats:** which apps get installed or opened through Tech Kadak, with no name, email or phone number. You can turn this off in **Settings > Usage stats bhejo**.
- **Random install ID:** a random ID made on your phone, with your phone model and Android and app version. It is not linked to your identity. It does not scan the list of apps on your phone.
- **Login (optional):** if you log in with Google, email or phone, that account is used for your member number and for reviews. You can skip login.
- **Reviews and suggestions:** what you write in a review or in the suggestion form is stored so it can be read and moderated. Do not put private details in them.
- **In-app browser:** history and bookmarks stay on your phone. Incognito tabs are not saved.
