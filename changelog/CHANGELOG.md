## [v1.5.0](https://github.com/techkadak/techkadak-app/releases/tag/v1.5.0) - 2026-11-05 - Browser, Reviews, Member Card

- New: opening animation (can be switched off in Settings)
- New: Browser tab with search, tabs, downloads, bookmarks, history and incognito
- New: official screenshots on app pages
